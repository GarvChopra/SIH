UNIFIX — Privacy Policy

Operated by {{ glb.name }} ({{ glb.short }}). This policy describes what the UNIFIX campus infrastructure-reporting application collects and how it is used. Draft for institute review — confirm the contact details and legal entity before publishing.

Who this is for

UNIFIX is an internal tool for {{ glb.short }} staff to report campus infrastructure issues (electrical, plumbing, civil, mechanical, power, IT/network) and for the campus administration to track them to resolution. Employees sign in with their Google account — a first sign-in automatically creates an employee account from that Google account's name and email address; there is no separate registration form. Administrator accounts are created by the campus administrator directly and still sign in with a username and PIN.

What we collect

DataWhy
Your name, email address and department To identify who reported an issue and to notify you of progress. For employees, your name and email come from your Google account on first sign-in.
Google Sign-InEmployees authenticate with their Google account via Google Sign-In. UNIFIX never sees or stores your Google password — Google confirms your identity and shares only your name, email address and email-verification status. Administrator accounts use a username and PIN instead, stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash — never in readable form.
Issue reports you submit: description text, photo, location (building / floor / room / facility), the priority you set, and when you noticed the issue To triage, route and resolve the issue, and to build campus-wide maintenance statistics.
Timestamps and a history of actions taken on each report (status changes, assignments, resolution notes and photos) Accountability and an audit trail for the administration.
A sign-in session cookie (HttpOnly)To keep you signed in. No third-party or advertising cookies are used.

UNIFIX does not collect your device location (GPS), contacts, calendar, microphone audio, or any advertising identifier. It contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Camera and photos

The app asks for camera / photo access only when you choose to attach a photo to a report. The photo is uploaded with that report and is visible to the campus administrator and maintenance staff handling the issue. Photos are downscaled before storage.

Where your data goes

  • {{ glb.short }} servers / database — all reports, accounts and history are stored here.
  • Groq, Inc. (AI classification) — only if enabled by the institute. When enabled, the text and photo of a new report are sent to Groq's API to suggest a category and severity. If not enabled, classification is done on-server with no external call.
  • Resend (email delivery) — only if enabled by the institute. When enabled, your contact email and the report reference are used to send you status-update emails.

Data is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

How long we keep it

Reports and their history are retained by the institute for as long as needed for maintenance record-keeping. Account data is retained while your account is active. See Account & Data Deletion to request removal.

Your choices

  • You can change your PIN in the app under Profile.
  • You can request that your account and the personal data linked to it be deleted — see Account & Data Deletion.
  • You can decline camera access; you simply won't be able to attach a photo.

Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. PINs are bcrypt-hashed. Sign-in cookies are HttpOnly and, in production, Secure. Administrative actions are access-controlled and audit-logged.

Children

UNIFIX is for institute staff and is not directed to children.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced in the app.
